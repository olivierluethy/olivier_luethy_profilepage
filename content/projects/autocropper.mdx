Turning one logo into every icon size an app or website needs is a chore that
usually spans five tools — remove.bg, Photoshop, a cropper, a resizer. AutoCropper
replaces that with a single drag-and-drop: drop a PNG, JPG or SVG and it returns a
full icon set from 16px to 512px in about five seconds.

## Everything runs on your device

Background detection, edge cleanup and downscaling all happen in the browser —
there are **no uploads**. The whole thing is built on Next.js, Tailwind and a
single `<canvas>`, in plain JavaScript: no WebAssembly blobs, no third-party AI
APIs. Your logo never leaves the tab.

## Monetization

A free tier handles 5 logos a day; Pro (CHF 5/month) removes the limit and adds
custom icon sizes.
