Most grammar checkers fix your sentence and move on. Grammar Mentor is built on
the opposite premise: the point is not the corrected sentence, it is that you
stop making the mistake. So every correction comes with the rule behind it.

## What it does

- Explains the grammatical logic behind each fix, in context.
- Suggests synonyms tuned to a chosen tone.
- Adapts to writing styles — neutral, formal, casual, academic, creative,
  professional, conversational — and tones from confident to concise.
- Lets you save snippets and gives personalized tips over time.
- Works across 15+ languages.

## Access

Free to try without an account (one AI explanation a day), more for registered
users, and a paid tier for heavier use.
