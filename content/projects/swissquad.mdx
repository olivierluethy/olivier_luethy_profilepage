Swissquad was an online shop for FPV drones and parts that I ran through 2024 and
2025 — the commercial side of the hobby I build and fly for myself. It gave me a
first-hand look at the parts of a product that have nothing to do with code:
sourcing, stock, storefront and getting an order to someone's door.

The shop is no longer online, so there is nothing left to link to — this entry is
here for the honest record rather than as a live storefront.
