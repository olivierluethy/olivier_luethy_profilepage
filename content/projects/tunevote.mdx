TuneVote is a music voting platform: a room votes on what plays next, in real
time, and the platform suggests songs to add.

## The moving parts

- **Real-time voting** over Socket.io, so every participant sees the queue update
  as votes come in rather than on a refresh.
- **AI song suggestions** through the OpenAI API, to seed and extend a queue when
  the room runs dry.
- **YouTube integration** via yt-dlp for resolving tracks.
- **Sign-in** with Google and Facebook.

It runs in Docker on a VPS, with database schema changes handled through Knex
migrations rather than by hand.
