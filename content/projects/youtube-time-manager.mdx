YouTube Time Manager tells you where your time on YouTube actually goes — how
long you spend per video and per channel, sorted into categories you define. 20+
people use it weekly.

## How it works

Background scripts measure watch time at the video and channel level, allowing
for tab switches and pauses so the numbers stay honest. You can cluster channels
into categories — Education, Productivity, Entertainment — and the extension
aggregates the totals on the fly into a small local dashboard of daily and
weekly stats and category breakdowns. It's built on Manifest V3 and is
local-first: everything lives in Chrome Storage / IndexedDB, nothing leaves the
browser. Open-source on [GitHub](https://github.com/BaskLash/YouTube-Time-Manager).
