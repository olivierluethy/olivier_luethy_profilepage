Planning a trip with friends usually happens across a group chat, a shared note,
and a reckoning with a spreadsheet afterwards. Trip xChange pulls all of that into
one place: shared itinerary decisions, a live activity schedule, photo sharing
that syncs across the group, and real-time expense logging with automatic
settlement — the app works out who owes whom.

Built in Switzerland, free to start, and set up in about half a minute.

## My role

I led the project as frontend and feature owner, and ran a Google Ads campaign
that brought in the first 20–30 international users to test it against real trips
rather than imagined ones.
