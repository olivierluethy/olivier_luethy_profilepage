Fitness platforms lock your activity data inside one company's app. FitPub takes
the opposite stance: it publishes your workouts to the **Fediverse** over
ActivityPub, so your training lives on an open, interoperable network rather than
in a silo — the same way Mastodon relates to a closed social network.

## What it does

- Log activities by hand, file upload or batch import, with a Komoot integration.
- A public timeline to discover and follow other people's activity.
- Personal analytics — dashboards and heatmaps.
- Data export, because it is your data.

It is open source, hosted on Codeberg, and federates with the wider Fediverse
rather than standing alone.
