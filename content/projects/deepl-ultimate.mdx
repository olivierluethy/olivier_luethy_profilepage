DeepL Ultimate automates translation and text-improvement work in DeepL, and
batches it — instead of pasting one block at a time, you can run many
translations at once and push a large volume of text through in a single
operation.

## How it works

The extension sits on top of DeepL and drives the translation flow for you,
queuing text so long documents don't have to be split by hand. It's published
on the Chrome Web Store as _DeepL Pro Unlimited — Long Texts Without Limits_.
