LinkedOut removes the parts of LinkedIn that are there to keep you scrolling —
the home feed, network recommendations and the notification red dots — so what's
left is the tool, not the time-sink. 40+ people use it weekly.

## How it works

It works by removing those elements from the page as they load, and it goes one
step further: if you try to open a distracting section by typing the URL
directly, it intercepts the request and redirects you back. There's a deliberate
design decision behind it — no popup, no on/off toggles. It just runs in the
background, which keeps it lightweight and means there's nothing to fiddle with.
Everything stays local; it collects no data.
